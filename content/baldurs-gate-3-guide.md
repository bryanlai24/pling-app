### Forged in Blood and Fire 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MILESTONE_FORGED`
- **title:** Complete the Adamantine Forge in the Underdark
- **method:** The Adamantine Forge is in the Grymforge area of the Underdark. You need two things: a Mould (found throughout Grymforge) and Mithral Ore (two veins are in the forge area). Insert both into the forge, pull the lever, then defeat Grym — the forge's guardian — to claim your item. **You can only forge two items per playthrough** (one per Mithral Ore vein), so choose your moulds wisely.

### Hero of the Forgotten Realms 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MILESTONE_HERO`
- **title:** Complete the game with a good ending
- **method:** Destroy the Netherbrain and refuse any offer of power for yourself. At the final confrontation, do not elect to become an illithid, do not claim the brain in Bhaal's name, and do not use the Netherstones to dominate the brain — just destroy it. This is the default heroic ending.

## Companion Questlines

> Full step-by-step for each companion's personal achievement. Keep the companion in your active party during their key story beats — some steps will not trigger if they are left at camp.

### Just a Nibble (Astarion) 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COMPANION_ASTARION`
- **title:** Allow Astarion to drink your blood
- **method:** Recruit Astarion from the Ravaged Beach in Act 1. During a long rest at camp, he will approach you at night and reveal he is a vampire, asking to feed. Allow him. He will go too far — pass either a DC 5 Persuasion or DC 7 Strength check to stop him before it becomes fatal. **Do not attack him** when he reveals himself or the moment is lost.

---

### Taking Blood (Astarion) 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COMPANION_ASTARION2`
- **title:** Complete Astarion's companion questline
- **method:** Astarion's questline culminates in Act 3 at Cazador's Palace in the Lower City of Baldur's Gate.

  **1. Build approval with Astarion throughout Acts 1 and 2.** He approves of cunning, self-serving, and morally flexible choices. Let him feed on enemies, support his autonomy, and avoid overly preachy responses.

  **2. Learn about Cazador.** Through camp conversations and Act 1 discoveries (the Szarr family signet, notes in the Underdark), piece together Astarion's history with his master.

  **3. Find Cazador's Palace in Act 3.** It is in the northwestern part of the Lower City. Enter and navigate through his vampiric servants.

  **4. Confront Cazador.** He will attempt to perform the Rite of Profane Ascension using Astarion and 7,000 vampire spawn. You can:
  - **Stop the ritual and kill Cazador** — Astarion remains a vampire spawn but is free. He may choose to spare or kill Cazador in a cutscene.
  - **Allow the ritual to complete** — Astarion ascends to a full vampire lord. This is a darker path but valid for an evil playthrough.

  **⚠️ If you pursue Astarion's romance**, the ascension path has significant relationship consequences. The free path leads to a more emotionally resolved conclusion.

---

### To Bloom in Darkest Night (Shadowheart) 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COMPANION_SHADOWHEART`
- **title:** Complete Shadowheart's companion questline
- **method:** Shadowheart's questline spans all three acts and resolves at the Gauntlet of Shar in Act 2 and the House of Grief in Act 3.

  **1. Rescue Shadowheart from the nautiloid pod (Act 1 prologue, optional).** If you save her on the nautiloid, she begins with higher approval.

  **2. Build approval and learn about her past.** Exhaust her camp dialogue. In Act 1, ask about her life beyond Shar to learn she cannot swim and loves night orchids.

  **3. Find the night orchid for Shadowheart (Act 2).** On the ledge immediately in front of the Ruined Battlefield waypoint in the Shadow-Cursed Lands, there is a night orchid among the alchemy ingredients — easy to walk past. Pick it up and speak to Shadowheart with it in your inventory.

  **4. Complete the Gauntlet of Shar (Act 2).** The Gauntlet is beneath the Thorm Mausoleum in the Shadow-Cursed Lands. Navigate its trials. At the end, Shadowheart must make a choice about the Spear of Night and her faith.

  **5. Resolve the Nightsong.** At the end of the Gauntlet, Shadowheart can either:
  - **Free the Nightsong (Dame Aylin)** — Shadowheart rejects Shar and begins her own path. This is the good-aligned resolution.
  - **Kill the Nightsong** — Shadowheart becomes Shar's Dark Justiciar. Leads to a darker but still completable questline.

  **6. Confront Shadowheart's parents at the House of Grief (Act 3).** The House of Grief is in the Lower City. Shadowheart's parents are held prisoner by the Sharrans. Depending on her Act 2 choice, she can save them or be made to kill them.

  **⚠️ Missable:** The night orchid in Act 2 is the most commonly missed step. It is a standard alchemy ingredient with no special marker — check the ground carefully at the Ruined Battlefield waypoint.

### No Free Lunches (Gale) 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COMPANION_GALE`
- **title:** Complete Gale's companion questline
- **method:** Gale's questline involves feeding his magical hunger and preventing — or enabling — his catastrophic contingency plan.

  **1. Feed Gale magical items regularly (Acts 1 and 2).** He needs to consume magical artifacts to suppress his Netherese orb. Offer him items when he asks — he needs roughly 3 across Act 1. Items are consumed permanently, so give him weaker pieces you don't need. **Keeping him unfed causes his condition to worsen and can eventually kill him.**

  **2. Meet Elminster at camp (Act 2).** Elminster arrives during a long rest in Act 2 and partially heals Gale's condition, reducing (but not eliminating) his need to feed.

  **3. Receive the Crown of Karsus vision.** Elminster delivers a message from Mystra: Gale is to detonate himself to destroy the Absolute. Exhaust Gale's camp dialogue as this develops — he shares his past and his relationship with Mystra.

  **4. Resolve Gale's fate in Act 3.** At the final confrontation, Gale has several options:
  - **Detonate the orb** — destroys the Netherbrain but kills Gale (and possibly your party).
  - **Use the Crown of Karsus** — Gale claims godhood for himself. An ambiguous but powerful ending.
  - **Survive with your party** — if you destroy the brain together, Gale survives and reconciles with Mystra.

### The Lich-Queen's Wrath (Lae'zel) 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COMPANION_LAEZL`
- **title:** Become an enemy of Vlaakith and ally with Prince Voss
- **method:** This achievement is tied to Lae'zel's Act 2 questline at the Githyanki Creche.

  **1. Recruit Lae'zel** from the cage in Act 1 (convince her captors to free her, or break her out).

  **2. Follow Lae'zel's lead toward the Creche.** She urgently wants to reach a Githyanki Creche to be cured of the tadpole. The Creche Y'llek is in the Mountain Pass area.

  **3. Complete the Creche.** Navigate through the Creche with Lae'zel in your party. Reach the Inquisitor's Chamber and defeat the Inquisitor in combat.

  **4. Speak with Vlaakith in the Astral Prism.** After the Inquisitor is defeated, Vlaakith contacts you via the Astral Prism and orders you to kill your Guardian inside it. **Refuse and do not kill your Guardian.** This makes you an enemy of Vlaakith.

  **5. Accept Voss's alliance at camp.** Prince Voss — a Githyanki knight who was speaking with your Guardian — comes to your camp after the Creche. Accept his offer of alliance against Vlaakith. The achievement triggers here.

  **⚠️ Do not attack or antagonize Voss at the Mountain Pass earlier in Act 2** — he appears on the road and must be allowed to leave peacefully.

---

### You Have Two Hands for a Reason (Karlach) 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COMPANION_KARLACH`
- **title:** Complete Karlach's companion questline
- **method:** Karlach is a tiefling Barbarian with an infernal engine for a heart that burns too hot to touch. Her quest is to repair it enough to survive — and to find the man who sent her to Avernus.

  **1. Recruit Karlach** near the Risen Road in Act 1 — Wyll is hunting her, believing her to be a devil. Side with Karlach.

  **2. Find an Infernal Iron.** Karlach's heart needs infernal materials to cool down. Multiple Infernal Irons are found throughout Act 1 (Shattered Sanctum goblin camp, Blighted Village basement, etc.).

  **3. Bring the Infernal Iron to Dammon.** Dammon is a tiefling smith at the Emerald Grove (and later at the Last Light Inn in Act 2). He can work on Karlach's engine. **He must survive Act 1 — if the Grove falls to the goblins, Dammon dies and Karlach's quest cannot be completed.**

  **4. Repair Karlach's heart twice.** A second Infernal Iron and a second visit to Dammon (in Act 2 at Last Light Inn) allows full physical contact. Exhaust Karlach's camp dialogue after each repair.

  **5. Confront Gortash (Act 3).** Karlach's former master is Lord Enver Gortash, one of the Absolute's three chosen. His downfall is part of the main story — Karlach gets closure through his defeat.

  **6. Resolve Karlach's ending.** Her engine cannot be fixed permanently without returning to Avernus. At the end of the game, she can:
  - Return to Avernus (alone or with a companion).
  - Die in Faerûn.
  - Become a mind flayer to survive (only if you pursue that path).

---

### Rude, Crude, and Full of Attitude (Wyll) 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COMPANION_WYLL`
- **title:** Complete Wyll's companion questline
- **method:** Wyll is a warlock whose pact with the devil Mizora has cost him more than he bargained for. His questline is about breaking — or fulfilling — that pact.

  **1. Recruit Wyll** at the Emerald Grove in Act 1. He is training tiefling refugees.

  **2. Meet Mizora at camp (Act 1 long rest).** After recruiting Wyll, Mizora appears at camp and interacts with him. Exhaust Wyll's dialogue about his past.

  **3. Follow Mizora's demands through Act 2.** Mizora reappears at the Last Light Inn, captured and imprisoned. She offers Wyll a choice: free her or let her die (which kills his father). Freeing her is required to progress his questline toward the good resolution.

  **4. Resolve Wyll's pact in Act 3.** At the Iron Throne (an underwater prison), Wyll's father Grand Duke Ravengard is imprisoned. Rescue him during the Iron Throne mission. Afterward, Mizora offers Wyll a final choice: renew his pact permanently to save his father, or break free. **Breaking the pact is the achievement-friendly path** — Wyll becomes the blade of Frontiers unbound.

  **5. See Wyll's story through to the epilogue.** Depending on choices, Wyll can become Duke of Baldur's Gate, continue adventuring, or rule Avernus with Karlach.

  **⚠️ The Iron Throne rescue is time-pressured** — you have a limited number of turns to free the prisoners before they drown. Bring a character with high movement speed and crowd control.

### Loophole (Wyll) 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COMPANION_WYLL2`
- **title:** Break Wyll's pact with Mizora
- **method:** See Wyll's questline above. At the final pact decision in Act 3, choose to break Wyll's pact with Mizora rather than renew it. This requires having rescued Grand Duke Ravengard from the Iron Throne first — if his father is dead, Wyll has no leverage and breaking the pact costs more.

### Hot Date (Karlach) 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COMPANION_KARLACH2`
- **title:** Go on a date with Karlach in the Lower City
- **method:** Requires pursuing Karlach's romance and completing both heart repairs (see Karlach's questline above). After reaching the Lower City in Act 3, speak to Karlach and select "It's been a long time since we last connected." She will suggest a date. During the next long rest, the date scene plays out. **Both heart repairs via Dammon must be completed before this becomes available.**

## Endings

> Each of these requires specific setup before the final confrontation with the Netherbrain. **None of these can be triggered after the point of no return** — once you enter the Netherbrain sequence, your ending is locked in based on prior choices.

### Mind Blown 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_MINDBLOWN`
- **title:** Use the Netherstones to dominate and control the Netherbrain
- **method:** At the final confrontation, rather than destroying the Netherbrain, use the Netherstones to dominate it and bend it to your will. This requires having all three Netherstones (obtained from defeating the three Chosen — Ketheric Thorm, Enver Gortash, Orin the Red). Select the option to command the brain rather than destroy it.

---

### Ceremorphosis 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_CEREMORPHOSIS`
- **title:** Complete the game as a mind flayer
- **method:** During a conversation with the Emperor in the Astral Prism (this option becomes available in Act 3), offer to consume a mind flayer parasite and transform. You must then complete the rest of the game — the Upper City assault, channeling Karsus's power into the Netherbrain, and the epilogue — as a mind flayer. **This is a permanent in-playthrough transformation.** The Emperor presents this option as a way to gain power against the Absolute.

---

### Embrace Your Urge 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_EMBRACEURGE`
- **title:** Accept the Slayer form from the Dark Urge
- **method:** This achievement is only available when playing as the Dark Urge origin character. During the confrontation with Orin the Red in Act 3, she will offer to let you embrace your murderous nature and become the Slayer — a powerful bhaalspawn form. Accept her offer. Orin then fights you as a human rather than transforming herself, which changes the fight dynamic significantly.

---

### Absolute Power Corrupts 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_ABSOLUTEPOWER`
- **title:** Become the Absolute
- **method:** At the final confrontation with the Netherbrain, choose to claim it for yourself — taking control of the Absolute's power and ruling as its master. This requires having completed enough of the evil path to be presented with this option. Combining it with the Dark Urge playthrough (claiming the brain in Bhaal's name) leads to the **Sins of the Father** achievement.

---

### Sins of the Father 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_SINSFATHER`
- **title:** Claim the Netherbrain in Bhaal's name as the Dark Urge
- **method:** Playing as the Dark Urge origin character, fully embrace your bhaalspawn nature throughout the game. At the final confrontation, select the option to claim the Netherbrain in Bhaal's name — dedicating its power to the god of murder. Requires having unlocked the Slayer form (either through the Orin confrontation in Act 3 or by dueling her) and not having rejected your Dark Urge nature at the Bhaal Temple in Act 3.

## Missables & Specific Encounters

> These all require knowing exactly when and where to act. Most are easy once you know what to do — the difficulty is just awareness.

### Leave No One Behind 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISSABLE_LEAVENONE`
- **title:** Save all Tiefling refugees across all three acts
- **method:** This is the most complex missable achievement in the game. A specific set of Tieflings must survive all three acts.

  **Act 1 — Save the Emerald Grove:**
  - Side with the Tieflings against Minthara and the goblins. Defeat all three goblin leaders (Priestess Gut, Dror Razglin, Minthara) at the Shattered Sanctum.
  - **Do not let Kagha complete the Rite of Thorns** — find the evidence of her Shadow Druid ties in the wooden chest in her room to expose her before the ritual is finalized.
  - Free Lae'zel from her cage without killing her Tiefling captors — pass the dialogue checks or use non-lethal means.
  - Ensure Rolan and his siblings (Cal and Lia) stay with the refugees for the journey — convince Rolan not to leave them behind.

  **Act 2 — Defend the Last Light Inn:**
  - When you speak with Isobel at the Last Light Inn, an attack triggers immediately. **Defend the inn and keep Isobel alive** — if she falls, the inn's protection drops and Tieflings die.
  - Save Rolan: he is found trapped at the Ruined Battlefield (coordinates X: -54, Y: -111). Rescue him before he is killed.
  - Rescue Zevlor: he is found in the Illithid Colony beneath Moonrise Towers. He must be freed from the mind flayer pod.

  **Act 3 — No additional Tiefling steps required.**

  **⚠️ Dark Urge warning:** The Dark Urge has a scripted murder of Alfira, a Tiefling bard, after long rests. To prevent this, knock Alfira unconscious before each long rest after meeting her in Act 1 — this causes a different NPC (Quil) to be the victim instead, preserving Alfira and the achievement.

  **⚠️ Note:** Several Tieflings die in the Shadow-Cursed Lands regardless of your actions (Amek, Asharak, Elegis, and others) — these deaths do not affect the achievement.

### She Cannot Be Caged! 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISSABLE_CAGED`
- **title:** Rescue Sazza the goblin from the Emerald Grove, Goblin Camp, and Moonrise Towers
- **method:** Sazza is a goblin prisoner in the Emerald Grove in Act 1. You must rescue her three times across three locations.

  **1. Free Sazza from the Grove prison (Act 1).** She is in a cell in the Emerald Grove — pick the lock or find the key. Speak to her and agree to help her escape. Guide her out through the grove without her being killed by the Tieflings.

  **2. Find Sazza at the Goblin Camp (Act 1).** After escaping the Grove, Sazza returns to the Shattered Sanctum. Find her there and speak with her again.

  **3. Find and free Sazza at Moonrise Towers (Act 2).** Sazza is imprisoned again at Moonrise Towers in Act 2. Locate her cell and free her a third time to trigger the achievement.

  **⚠️ Siding with the Tieflings and wiping out the goblin leaders in Act 1 makes reaching Sazza at the Shattered Sanctum more difficult — plan your approach to the camp accordingly if you want this achievement.**

### A Grym Fate 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISSABLE_GRYM`
- **title:** Defeat the Adamantine Golem Grym using the forge's own hammer
- **method:** Grym is the guardian of the Adamantine Forge in the Grymforge (Underdark). The intended method — and the one that triggers this achievement — is to use the forge's environment: lure Grym onto the central platform, activate the forge (pull the lever to heat it with lava), wait for Grym to be superheated (he glows orange), then have a character pull the overhead hammer lever to smash him. Repeat until defeated. **Using the hammer while Grym is superheated deals massive damage — standard attacks and spells are mostly ineffective against him otherwise.**

### Non-Invasive Procedure 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISSABLE_SURGEON`
- **title:** Kill the Surgeon before he can operate on you
- **method:** The Surgeon (Lenore de Hurst) is a boss in the Lower City of Baldur's Gate (Act 3), found in a hidden location connected to the Bhaalist murders investigation. When the fight begins she attempts to perform surgery on a party member. Kill her before she completes her first surgical action. Burst damage and crowd control at the start of combat are essential — focus her immediately on turn one.

### Penny Pincher 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISSABLE_PENNYPINCHER`
- **title:** Defeat the Toll Collector without letting her use gold against you
- **method:** The Toll Collector is a boss in the Counting House vault in Act 3. Her primary mechanic is animating and hurling gold coins as a weapon. To prevent her from using gold: **destroy or move the coin piles in her room before initiating combat**, or kill her fast enough on turn one that she never gets an action. Dimension Door and Misty Step can be used to reposition away from coin piles mid-combat. High initiative and burst damage are the most reliable approach.

### Crash Landing 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISSABLE_CRASHLANDING`
- **title:** Knock the dragon out of the sky mid-flight in the Wyrmway
- **method:** The Wyrmway is accessed through the Wyrm's Rock Prison in Act 3 (look for hidden switches behind two paintings in the prison — each reveals a button; press both). Inside, a trial involves a dragon — Ansur — that takes to the air during combat. **Wait until Ansur is mid-flight**, then knock him out of the sky using a knockback or forced movement ability (Eldritch Blast with Repelling Blast, Thunderwave, Shove, etc.). Timing matters — he has to be visibly airborne, not just leaping.

### Fancy Footwork 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISSABLE_FANCYFOOTWORK`
- **title:** Defeat Gortash at Wyrm's Rock without triggering any traps
- **method:** Lord Enver Gortash is fought at Wyrm's Rock Fortress in Act 3. The fortress is loaded with Flaming Fist traps and pressure plates. **Disarm every trap before initiating the boss fight** — use a high-Perception character to spot them and a high-Dexterity character (or the Disarm Trap skill) to neutralize them. Gortash himself can trigger traps during combat, so clear the room completely before engaging. Detect Thoughts and careful exploration before combat are essential.

### Devil's in the Details 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISSABLE_DEVILDETAILS`
- **title:** Defeat Commander Zhalk on the nautiloid before escaping
- **method:** Commander Zhalk is an enemy on the nautiloid during the prologue. The prologue is designed to be escaped, not fought — Zhalk is a high-level enemy most players run past. To kill him: assist the mind flayer (Us) who is already fighting Zhalk, use the environment (explosives, pushing), and burn Zhalk's HP down before the escape timer forces you out. **You have a limited number of turns before the nautiloid sequence ends.** Focus all damage on Zhalk, not the other enemies. This is significantly easier with a combat-optimized build.

---

### Escapologist 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_ESCAPOLOGIST`
- **title:** Break out of prison after being arrested
- **method:** You can get arrested through various means in Acts 2 and 3 — pickpocketing in Moonrise Towers, being caught stealing in Baldur's Gate, or certain quest outcomes. Once imprisoned, escape without using a key. Picking the cell lock (requires Thieves' Tools and a decent Sleight of Hand), breaking the door, or using magic like Dimension Door or Misty Step to teleport out all count. **The most consistent method:** get arrested in Baldur's Gate in Act 3 and use Thieves' Tools on the cell lock.

## Challenge / Difficulty

### Critical Hit 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_CHALLENGE_TACTICIAN`
- **title:** Complete the game on Tactician difficulty
- **method:** Select Tactician difficulty at the start of a new game (or switch to it in the options before finishing — the achievement checks your difficulty at the point of completion). Tactician increases enemy HP, damage, and AI aggression. Key tips:
  - **Short rest frequently** — you get two short rests per long rest, and they're free.
  - **High ground is critical** — attacks from elevation get a significant bonus to hit. Always contest and hold the high ground.
  - **Crowd control wins fights** — Sleep, Hold Person, and Web are extremely powerful on Tactician, as disabled enemies are trivially killable.
  - **Consumables are your friends** — alchemical solutions, arrows, and scrolls that feel wasteful on Normal are essential investments on Tactician.

---

### Foehammer 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_CHALLENGE_HONOURMODE`
- **title:** Complete the game on Honour Mode
- **method:** Honour Mode is a single-save, single-life run — if your entire party dies, the save file is permanently retired to a Hall of Honour. Bosses have powerful Legendary Actions unique to this mode. This is BG3's ultimate challenge.

  **Before you start:**
  - Complete at least one full playthrough on Tactician first. Know every act's major fights and story beats before risking an Honour run.
  - Play a practice run on Custom difficulty with all Honour Mode rules enabled to learn the Legendary Actions without risking your save.

  **General principles:**
  - **Speed kills** — every Honour boss fight should be ended as fast as possible. Legendary Actions accrue value the longer a fight drags on. Going from 100% to 0 in one turn is the goal; prepare burst damage setups accordingly.
  - **Read Legendary Actions before engaging** — use the Examine action on every boss at the start of combat to read their Legendary Action descriptions. Surprises in Honour Mode can be fatal.
  - **Avoid cheese if it could backfire** — some exploits that work on lower difficulties can fail unpredictably. Safe, reliable strategies are worth more than risky high-damage combos.
  - **Never rush into a new area unprepared** — take a long rest before every major boss fight, regardless of resources remaining.

  **Act-by-act watch-outs:**
  - **Act 1:** Auntie Ethel and the Phase Spider Matriarch have dangerous Legendary Actions. The Hag fight can teleport characters into hazards.
  - **Act 2:** Ketheric Thorm's Legendary Actions make his second phase (the Mind Flayer Colony fight) particularly brutal. The Glabrezu in the Gauntlet of Shar is a surprise threat.
  - **Act 3:** The Netherbrain fight has Legendary Actions that can one-shot underleveled characters. Gortash's trap-filled arena is a major attrition risk. The Ansur dragon fight is deceptively difficult.

  **⚠️ Honour Mode does not allow multiclassing into every class in one playthrough** — that would require too many respecs, each of which risks resource attrition. Focus your build from the start.

### No Penny Required 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_DETECTTHOUGHTS`
- **title:** Successfully use Detect Thoughts on an NPC
- **method:** Cast the Detect Thoughts spell during dialogue with any NPC who has hidden thoughts. The spell is available to Wizards, Sorcerers, and Bards by default; Shadowheart can also cast it. Many NPCs in Act 1 have detectable thoughts — try it on the merchant at the Emerald Grove or the goblins at the Shattered Sanctum.

### Dig for Victory 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_DIG`
- **title:** Dig up five buried chests in a single playthrough
- **method:** Buried chests are scattered throughout the world — your character with the highest Perception will spot them automatically as glowing dig spots. They are plentiful in Act 1 around the Blighted Village, Druid Grove, and Underdark. A shovel is required to dig (found commonly throughout the world).

### Roleplayer 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_ROLEPLAYER`
- **title:** Complete ten background goals in a single playthrough
- **method:** Each character background (Noble, Urchin, Soldier, etc.) has passive goals that trigger automatically when you do relevant things. For example, a Noble background character earns a goal by persuading someone through status, and a Criminal earns one by picking locks. Check your Journal for your current background and its associated actions, and play into it during Act 1 — ten goals across a full playthrough is very achievable without trying.

### Action Surge 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_ACTIONSURGE`
- **title:** Perform five attacks in one turn
- **method:** Requires a Fighter with Extra Attack and the Action Surge class feature (available at Fighter level 2 for Action Surge, level 5 for Extra Attack). On your turn: use your Action to attack twice (Extra Attack), then use Action Surge to attack twice more, plus your Bonus Action attack (if applicable via Two-Weapon Fighting or a subclass). Five attacks in one turn is easiest with a level 5+ Fighter.

### Shove Off 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_SHOVEOFF`
- **title:** Kill an enemy with falling damage
- **method:** Shove a character off a ledge in combat (Shove is a free bonus action for all characters). Any ledge with a drop that deals fall damage counts — the Goblin Camp, the Underdark, and the Blighted Village all have easy opportunities. Position an enemy near a ledge and shove. **Ensure the fall is lethal**, not just damaging — higher drops and already-weakened enemies make this easier.

### Fists of Fury 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_FISTS`
- **title:** Kill an enemy with an Unarmed Strike
- **method:** Unequip both weapons and attack any enemy with a basic Unarmed Strike. Monks do this naturally and are the easiest class for this — their Unarmed Strikes deal decent damage. For other classes, weaken an enemy first with spells or weapons, then land the killing blow bare-handed.

### Bookworm 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_BOOKWORM`
- **title:** Read 100 different books in a single playthrough
- **method:** Open and read (not just loot) 100 unique books. Books are everywhere — bookshelves in the Emerald Grove, the Underdark, the Arcane Tower, and throughout Baldur's Gate are all stacked with them. Click to open each book and let the text load, then close. 100 is very achievable across a full playthrough if you make a habit of reading every bookshelf you encounter. **The Arcane Tower in the Underdark and Sorcerous Sundries in Act 3 are particularly dense.**

### Fetch Quest 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_FETCH`
- **title:** Play fetch with Scratch the dog
- **method:** Find Scratch the dog near the Blighted Village in Act 1 (he is sitting by a dead body on the road). Pass the Animal Handling or Speak with Animals check to befriend him and send him to camp. At camp, interact with Scratch and initiate a game of fetch by throwing a ball. He will return it. **Speak with Animals is the most reliable way to recruit him** if your Wisdom is not high enough for Animal Handling.

**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_BOTTOMSUP`

- **method:** When setting up a Long Rest, remove all food supplies from the camp chest and replace them entirely with alcoholic drinks (wine, ale, spirits — found throughout the world). Initiate the Long Rest with only alcohol as the resource. Stock up on alcohol in Act 1 — taverns and the Shattered Sanctum have plenty.

### Homebrewer 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_HOMEBREWER`

- **method:** Open the Alchemy panel (available from inventory) and combine ingredients to brew solutions. Each unique formula you craft for the first time counts. Common early recipes: Antidote (Mugwort + any salt), Elixir of Arcane Cultivation (Vitriol of Shadow + any sublimate). Collect alchemy ingredients throughout Act 1 — they are abundant in the Underdark and on most bodies.

### Jack-of-all-Trades 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_JACKOFALLTRADES`
- **title:** Multiclass into every class in one playthrough without using Withers to change your class
- **method:** There are 12 classes in BG3. You must take at least one level in each — all in a single playthrough — without ever using Withers's respec service to remove classes. Plan your level-up progression from character creation: start as one class, and add new classes every time you level up, prioritizing classes you haven't taken yet. You will be a highly ineffective character — this is intentional. **12 classes means your character will cap out around level 12 with 1 level in each**, just as you reach the end of the game. Do not respec, as this removes existing class levels and breaks the achievement.

### Punch Drunk 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_PUNCHDRUNK`
- **title:** Defeat 20 enemies while drunk in a single playthrough
- **method:** Consume alcohol before or during combat to gain the Drunk condition, then kill enemies while it is active. Alcohol is abundant and cheap. The Drunk condition is gained by drinking any alcoholic item — it provides a small Charisma bonus and imposes Disadvantage on Dexterity saves. Any kill while the condition is active counts. Stack this throughout the playthrough rather than grinding it in one session.

---

### Busker 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MISC_BUSKER`
- **title:** Earn 100 gold from busking in a single playthrough
- **method:** Equip a musical instrument (Lute, Lyre, Drum, etc. — found throughout the world), find a busy public area with foot traffic (the Emerald Grove or any populated area in Baldur's Gate), and use the Perform action repeatedly. Each performance earns a small amount of gold from nearby NPCs. **This takes a lot of repetitions** — it is easiest to set this up in Act 3 in a densely populated area of Baldur's Gate and just keep performing between other activities until you hit 100 gold total.
