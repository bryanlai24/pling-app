## Endings

> All endings are triggered at the same point — after defeating the Elden Beast, interact with the fractured Marika to choose your ending. **Every ending except Elden Lord requires quest steps completed before this fog gate.** If you want multiple ending achievements, you need multiple playthroughs (or use a save backup before the final boss).
>
> The default ending (Elden Lord) requires nothing beyond beating the game. All other endings require specific questline completion beforehand.

---

### Elden Lord 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_ELDENLORD`
- **title:** Achieve the Elden Lord ending — mend the Elden Ring
- **method:** This is the default ending. After defeating the Elden Beast, approach the fractured Marika and select "Mend the Elden Ring." No prior quest steps required. If you have completed none of the other ending questlines, this is your only option.

---

### Age of the Stars 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_AGESTARS`
- **title:** Achieve the Age of Stars ending — follow Ranni's summon sign
- **method:** Requires completing Ranni's questline in full (see NPC Questlines section). After the Elden Beast is defeated, Ranni's summon sign appears on the ground near Marika instead of the usual option. Interact with it to trigger the Age of Stars ending.

  **⚠️ The questline must be fully completed before you enter the Elden Beast fog gate.** The final step — giving Ranni the Dark Moon Ring at the Cathedral of Manus Celes on the Moonlight Altar — must be done in advance.

---

### Lord of Frenzied Flame 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_FRENZY`
- **title:** Achieve the Lord of Frenzied Flame ending — release the Frenzied Flame
- **method:** Requires embracing the Three Fingers in the Cathedral of the Forsaken, the deepest point of the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds beneath Leyndell.

  **Steps to reach the Three Fingers:**
  1. Enter the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds via the well in Leyndell's Avenue Balcony area.
  2. Navigate to the bottom of the sewers, past the Mohg the Omen boss room.
  3. After Mohg's room, continue deeper into a collapsing tunnel section. Drop down through the wooden platforms — this is easy to miss as it looks like a dead end.
  4. At the bottom, remove all your armor and equipment (you must be completely unequipped) to open the door to the Three Fingers. Interact with them to be branded.

  After this, the Lord of Frenzied Flame ending becomes available at the conclusion of the game — approach Marika and it triggers automatically (you no longer have a choice unless you undo it).

  **⚠️ Embracing the Frenzied Flame locks you out of all other endings** unless you use Miquella's Needle to reverse it. Miquella's Needle is obtained by completing Millicent's questline, then using the Unalloyed Gold Needle at Malenia's arena. The Needle must then be used at the Dragonlord Placidusax arena in Crumbling Farum Azula to cleanse the flame branding. See the Millicent questline entry for full details.

---

### Age of Duskborn 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_DUSKBORN`
- **title:** Achieve the Age of Duskborn ending — use the Mending Rune of the Death-Prince
- **method:** Requires completing Fia's questline through to the end, including defeating Lichdragon Fortissax (see NPC Questlines section). Fia gives you the **Mending Rune of the Death-Prince** upon completion.

  After defeating the Elden Beast, approach Marika and select "Use Mending Rune of the Death-Prince" to trigger this ending.

  **⚠️ The Mending Rune must be in your inventory before entering the final fog gate.** Ensure you have completed Fia's quest and received the rune beforehand. Also requires the Cursemark of Death from Ranni's questline to give to Fia — you do not need to finish Ranni's quest, only reach the Divine Tower of Liurnia step.

---

### Blessing of Despair 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_DESPAIR`
- **title:** Achieve the Blessing of Despair ending — use the Mending Rune of the Fell Curse
- **method:** Requires completing the Dung Eater's questline by giving him five Seedbed Curses (see NPC Questlines section). He gives you the **Mending Rune of the Fell Curse** upon completion.

  After defeating the Elden Beast, approach Marika and select "Use Mending Rune of the Fell Curse" to trigger this ending.

  **⚠️ The Mending Rune must be in your inventory before entering the final fog gate.** Complete the Dung Eater's quest in advance. Note that the Sword of Milos (Legendary Armaments) is a drop from killing the Dung Eater, but completing his questline also awards it — do the quest, don't just kill him.

---

### Perfect Order 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_ENDING_ORDER`
- **title:** Achieve the Perfect Order ending — use the Mending Rune of Perfect Order
- **method:** Requires completing Goldmask's questline through to the end (see NPC Questlines section). Goldmask gives you the **Mending Rune of Perfect Order** at his final location in Leyndell, Ashen Capital.

  After defeating the Elden Beast, approach Marika and select "Use Mending Rune of Perfect Order" to trigger this ending.

  **⚠️ The Mending Rune must be in your inventory before entering the final fog gate.** The questline requires casting Law of Regression (37 Intelligence) in front of the Two Fingers in Leyndell — plan accordingly if your build is not Intelligence-focused. Temporarily boosting INT with talismans or a respec is sufficient.

## Collection Achievements

### Legendary Armaments 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COLLECT_ARMAMENTS`
- **title:** Collect all 9 Legendary Armaments
- **method:** You need all 9 legendary armaments in your inventory at the same time. You do not need to wield or upgrade them. Most are found in chests or as boss drops.

  1. **Grafted Blade Greatsword** — Dropped by Leonine Misbegotten, the boss of Castle Morne in the Weeping Peninsula.

  2. **Ruins Greatsword** — Dropped by the Misbegotten Warrior & Crucible Knight duo boss in Redmane Castle (accessible during the Radahn Festival or after Radahn is defeated).

  3. **Starscourge Greatsword** — Purchased from Enia at the Roundtable Hold after defeating Starscourge Radahn and obtaining his Great Rune.

  4. **Golden Order Greatsword** — Dropped by the Misbegotten Crusader in the Cave of the Forlorn in the Consecrated Snowfield (requires two Stonesword Keys to unlock the fog gate at the entrance).

  5. **Sword of Night and Flame** — In a chest inside Caria Manor (the main manor building, not the cliffs). From the Manor Lower Level site of grace, go south along the wall, drop to a roof below, and the chest is in a room at the end.

  6. **Sword of Milos** — Dropped by the Dung Eater upon his death (either his phantom at the Roundtable Hold or his real body in the sewers). **⚠️ Getting this weapon locks out completing the Dung Eater's questline.** If you want both the questline achievement and this weapon, complete the questline first — the weapon is awarded on completion.

  7. **Dark Moon Greatsword** — Reward for completing Ranni's questline (given automatically at the end of her quest in the Cathedral of Manus Celes).

  8. **Eclipse Shotel** — In a chest in Castle Sol in the Mountaintops of the Giants (inside the church building in the northeast part of the castle).

  9. **Bolt of Gransax** — On the giant replica of the Bolt of Gransax weapon leaning against a building in Leyndell, Royal Capital. From the Erdtree Sanctuary site of grace, head towards the dragon corpse and look down — the bolt is lying on a ledge below. **⚠️ Missable: this item disappears permanently once Leyndell becomes the Ashen Capital after defeating Maliketh.** Pick it up before entering Crumbling Farum Azula.

### Legendary Talismans 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_COLLECT_TALISMANS`
- **title:** Collect all 8 Legendary Talismans
- **method:** All 8 legendary talismans must be in your inventory at the same time.

  1. **Erdtree's Favor +2** — In the Ashen Capital version of Leyndell (after defeating Maliketh). Found on a root near the Putrid Avatar enemy in the outer area of the Ashen Capital. It replaces the Erdtree's Favor +1 from the base capital.

  2. **Radagon's Soreseal** — In a chest behind an imp fog gate (requires two Stonesword Keys) in Fort Faroth, Caelid.

  3. **Moon of Nokstella** — In a chest in Nokstella, Eternal City (Ainsel River underground area). Located in a building patrolled by a giant ant and two Silver Tears.

  4. **Dragoncrest Greatshield Talisman** — In a chest in Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree. From the Drainage Channel site of grace, head down into the rot pool area and look for a room guarded by a Cleanrot Knight.

  5. **Marika's Soreseal** — In a chest at the top of the Divine Tower of Elphael, accessible from within Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree.

  6. **Legendary Talisman of Sorcery (Graven-Mass Talisman)** — In a chest in the Albinauric Rise in the Consecrated Snowfield. The tower is locked by an imp puzzle — use two Fanged Imp Ashes to solve it (summon them near the imp statues at the base).

  7. **Old Lord's Talisman** — In a chest in Crumbling Farum Azula, in the building with the Farum Azula Dragon enemy (the large open area near the Dragon Temple site of grace).

  8. **Shard of Alexander** — Obtained by defeating Iron Fist Alexander in his final duel in Crumbling Farum Azula (part of Alexander's questline — see NPC Questlines section).

  7. **Founding Rain of Stars** — In a chest at the top of Heretical Rise in the Mountaintops of the Giants. **⚠️ The bridge leading to Heretical Rise is invisible.** From the site of grace nearest the tower, walk east along the cliff — there is an invisible walkway that leads around and up to the tower entrance. ## NPC Questlines

### Ranni the Witch 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_NPC_RANNI`
- **title:** Complete Ranni's questline
- **method:** Ranni's quest is the longest in the game and unlocks the Age of Stars ending. It spans four major underground areas and requires defeating several bosses along the way.

  **1. Meet Ranni at her rise.**
  Travel to Ranni's Rise in the Three Sisters area, northwest of Caria Manor. You must clear Caria Manor first (the boss is Royal Knight Loretta). Exhaust Ranni's dialogue at the top of the rise and agree to serve her.

  **2. Speak to Ranni's servants.**
  Descend the tower and speak to all three servants in the nearby rises: Blaidd (Ranni's Rise adjacent tower), Iji the War Counselor (Road to the Manor), and Seluvis (Seluvis's Rise).

  **3. Find Nokron, Eternal City.**
  Ranni asks you to find the hidden city of Nokron. **This requires defeating Starscourge Radahn first** — his death causes a meteor to open a crater in southern Limgrave near Fort Haight. Drop through the crater to reach Nokron.

  **4. Retrieve the Fingerslayer Blade.**
  In Nokron, make your way through the city to the Ancestral Woods and then Night's Sacred Ground. The Fingerslayer Blade is in a chest inside Night's Sacred Ground.

  **5. Give the Fingerslayer Blade to Ranni.**
  Return to Ranni's Rise and give her the blade. She will give you the Carian Inverted Statue in return.

  **6. Unlock the Divine Tower of Liurnia.**
  Use the Carian Inverted Statue at the Carian Study Hall in eastern Liurnia (the statue is on the desk inside the entrance). This inverts the hall — navigate through it to reach the Divine Tower of Liurnia and obtain the Cursemark of Death.

  **7. Reach Ainsel River Main.**
  Use the coffin in the Ainsel River Well (northwest Liurnia, near the Academy Gate Town) to travel to Ainsel River Main. Pick up the Miniature Ranni doll near the first Site of Grace.

  **8. Talk to the miniature Ranni.**
  At any Site of Grace in Ainsel River, select "Talk to miniature Ranni" repeatedly (you may need to rest and try multiple times) until she speaks. She will tell you to kill the Baleful Shadow.

  **9. Kill the Baleful Shadow.**
  Progress through Ainsel River Main to Nokstella, Eternal City, then continue past Nokstella to find the Baleful Shadow (a Blaidd-lookalike invader) near the Lake of Rot entrance. Defeat it.

  **10. Cross the Lake of Rot and reach Astel.**
  Navigate the Lake of Rot (use Preserving Boluses or the Flame, Cleanse Me incantation to manage Scarlet Rot buildup). At the far end, take the coffin to the Grand Cloister, then continue to fight Astel, Naturalborn of the Void.

  **11. Reach the Moonlight Altar.**
  After defeating Astel, take the elevator up to the Moonlight Altar plateau in southwest Liurnia.

  **12. Give Ranni the Dark Moon Ring.**
  The Dark Moon Ring is in a chest at the Cathedral of Manus Celes on the Moonlight Altar plateau. Place it on Ranni's finger when prompted. The quest is complete — the Age of Stars ending is now available to you.

  **⚠️ Missable:** Do not attack or kill Blaidd, Iji, or Seluvis before completing the quest. Seluvis will try to give you a puppet potion to use on Nepheli — you can refuse or ignore it without consequence.

### Fia, Deathbed Companion 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_NPC_FIA`
- **title:** Complete Fia's questline
- **method:** Fia's quest leads to the Lichdragon Fortissax fight and contributes to the Age of Duskborn ending.

  **1. Embrace Fia at the Roundtable Hold.**
  Speak to Fia (the woman by the bed) and allow her to hold you. This gives you the Baldachin's Blessing item and applies a minor max HP debuff until you use it. You can embrace her multiple times.

  **2. Obtain the Weathered Dagger.**
  After embracing Fia enough, she will ask you to hold a Weathered Dagger for her. Accept it.

  **3. Give the dagger to D, Hunter of the Dead.**
  D is seated near the Twin Maiden Husks at the Roundtable Hold. Give him the dagger. This sets the next step in motion (a cutscene will play when you return to the Hold after progressing).

  **4. Find Fia in the Deeproot Depths.**
  After D's fate is settled, Fia leaves the Hold. Find her in the Deeproot Depths, accessible via the Siofra Aqueduct (past the two Valiant Gargoyle bosses — take the coffin at the waterfall). She is near the Prince of Death's Throne site of grace.

  **5. Obtain the Cursemark of Death.**
  Fia needs the Cursemark of Death to complete her purpose. **This item is obtained via Ranni's questline** — specifically from the Divine Tower of Liurnia after inverting Carian Study Hall. If you have not done Ranni's quest yet, do that step now. Give Fia the Cursemark.

  **6. Enter Fia's Dream and defeat Lichdragon Fortissax.**
  After giving her the Cursemark, reload the area and speak to Fia again. She will be in a dreaming state — interact with her to enter the dream. **⚠️ Missable: do not give Fia the Weathered Dagger if you intend to fight Fortissax — once you do, it locks out the Fortissax fight.** Fight and defeat Lichdragon Fortissax.

  **7. Receive the Mending Rune of the Death-Prince.**
  After the fight, speak to Fia one final time to receive the Mending Rune of the Death-Prince. This item is used at the conclusion of the game to trigger the Age of Duskborn ending.

### Dung Eater 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_NPC_DUNGEATER`
- **title:** Complete the Dung Eater's questline
- **method:** The Dung Eater's quest is one of the darker storylines in the game and leads to the Blessing of Despair ending.

  **1. Find the Dung Eater at the Roundtable Hold.**
  After obtaining your first Finger Remedy (a key item from a body in Leyndell sewers — see step 3), the Dung Eater appears as a red phantom in his room at the Roundtable Hold (the locked room on the left past the smith). Exhaust his dialogue.

  **2. Find his physical body in the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds.**
  The Dung Eater's real body is imprisoned in the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds beneath Leyndell (enter via the well in the Avenue Balcony area). Navigate to the bottom of the dungeon and open his jail cell with the Sewer-Gaol Key, which is sold by the Blackguard Big Boggart NPC (see step 4 for Boggart's location).

  **3. Bring him Seedbed Curses.**
  The Dung Eater wants Seedbed Curses — items found on corpses throughout the game. There are more than enough to complete the quest. Locations include: Roundtable Hold (after his phantom appears, check behind his room), Volcano Manor, Leyndell, and others. Give him Seedbed Curses one at a time; after the 5th, the quest advances.

  **4. Note on Blackguard Big Boggart.**
  Boggart is found first near the Boilprawn Shack in Liurnia. Buy Boiled Prawns from him, then he relocates to the Outer Moat in Leyndell. The Sewer-Gaol Key is purchased from him at the Leyndell location. **Don't kill Boggart** before buying the key.

  **5. Receive the Mending Rune of the Fell Curse.**
  After giving all required Seedbed Curses, the Dung Eater will give you the Mending Rune of the Fell Curse. This unlocks the Blessing of Despair ending.

### Brother Corhyn & Goldmask 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_NPC_GOLDMASK`
- **title:** Complete Goldmask's questline
- **method:** This quest is shared between Brother Corhyn and the silent prophet Goldmask, and leads to the Erdtree's Favor ending.

  **1. Find Brother Corhyn at the Roundtable Hold.**
  Corhyn is the incantation trainer at the Hold. Exhaust his dialogue until he mentions searching for the prophet Goldmask.

  **2. Find Goldmask on the Altus Plateau.**
  Goldmask stands on a broken bridge segment in the Altus Plateau, northeast of the Erdtree's outer wall (near the Grand Lift of Dectus area). He does not speak — just stand near him. Tell Corhyn where Goldmask is.

  **3. Relocate to the Mountaintops of the Giants.**
  After the Forge of the Giants is accessible, both NPCs move to the Stargazers' Ruins in the Mountaintops of the Giants. Find them there and exhaust dialogue.

  **4. Solve Goldmask's riddle — use Law of Regression.**
  Goldmask poses a silent theological riddle about the Two Fingers. To answer it, you must cast the sorcery **Law of Regression** while standing in front of the Two Fingers in Leyndell (the chamber with the two giant fingers near the Erdtree Sanctuary). This triggers a cutscene revealing Radagon's secret. Report back to Goldmask.

  **5. Find them in Leyndell, Ashen Capital.**
  After Maliketh is defeated and Leyndell becomes the Ashen Capital, find Corhyn and Goldmask's final positions near the colosseum in Leyndell. Exhaust all dialogue.

  **6. Receive the Mending Rune of Perfect Order.**
  Goldmask gives you the Mending Rune of Perfect Order, which is used to trigger the Erdtree's Favor (Order) ending.

  **⚠️ Note:** Law of Regression is a sorcery that requires 37 Intelligence. If your build doesn't have that, you'll need to temporarily boost Intelligence (Stargazer Heirloom talisman, etc.) or respec.

### Hyetta 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_NPC_HYETTA`
- **title:** Complete Hyetta's questline
- **method:** Hyetta is a blind girl following visions of the flame. Her quest is tied to the Lord of Frenzied Flame ending and requires progressing the Frenzied Flame questline.

  **1. Give Hyetta a Shabriri Grape at Purified Ruins.**
  Hyetta first appears at Purified Ruins in west Liurnia. Bring her a Shabriri Grape (obtained from Edgar at Castle Morne, or from other Frenzied Flame enemies).

  **2. Give her a second Shabriri Grape at Gate Town North.**
  She relocates to the Liurnia Lake Shore, near the Gate Town North site of grace. Give her another Shabriri Grape.

  **3. Give her a third at Bellum Church.**
  She next appears at Bellum Church in northeast Liurnia. Give her a third Shabriri Grape.

  **4. Give her Fingerprint Grape at Frenzied Flame Village.**
  She moves to the outskirts of the Frenzied Flame Village in northeast Liurnia. The Fingerprint Grape (a unique variant) is dropped by Festering Fingerprint Vyke, who invades you at the village outskirts. Give it to her.

  **5. Meet her at the Cathedral of the Forsaken.**
  The final step requires you to embrace the Frenzied Flame yourself first. Reach the Cathedral of the Forsaken in the Subterranean Shunning-Grounds (the deepest point under Leyndell). **⚠️ Embracing the Frenzied Flame permanently locks you into the Lord of Frenzied Flame ending unless you use Miquella's Needle to reverse it later** (see Millicent's questline). After you embrace the flame, Hyetta appears at the site of grace here. Exhaust her dialogue to complete the quest.

### Millicent 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_NPC_MILLICENT`
- **title:** Complete Millicent's questline
- **method:** Millicent's quest spans the entire second half of the game across four regions. It is the most geographically spread questline and has several missable steps. It also provides the only way to undo the Frenzied Flame ending lock.

  **1. Obtain the Unalloyed Gold Needle from Commander O'Neil.**
  Commander O'Neil is the field boss of the Swamp of Aeonia in Caelid (enter the scarlet rot swamp). Defeat him to obtain the Unalloyed Gold Needle.

  **2. Give the needle to Gowry in Sellia.**
  Gowry is an old man in a shack on the outskirts of Sellia, Town of Sorcery in Caelid. Give him the needle; he'll ask you to first unlock Sellia's magic seals to access the church inside town.

  **3. Unlock Sellia's seals.**
  Light the three flame pillars visible on rooftops throughout Sellia (climb the ruins and jump between buildings). This opens the gates blocking the church. Exhaust Gowry's dialogue and collect the repaired needle.

  **4. Give the needle to Millicent at the Church of the Plague.**
  The Church of the Plague is atop the hill east of Sellia (you can jump up the cliffside). Give Millicent the repaired Unalloyed Gold Needle. Exhaust her dialogue.

  **5. Find Millicent at Erdtree-Gazing Hill in the Altus Plateau.**
  After advancing to the Altus Plateau, Millicent relocates to the Erdtree-Gazing Hill site of grace. Exhaust her dialogue.

  **6. Find Millicent at the Ancient Snow Valley Ruins in the Mountaintops of the Giants.**
  After reaching the Mountaintops of the Giants, find Millicent near the Ancient Snow Valley Ruins site of grace. Exhaust her dialogue.

  **7. Get the Valkyrie's Prosthesis from the Shaded Castle.**
  The Shaded Castle is in the northern Altus Plateau. Inside, find the Valkyrie's Prosthesis in a chest. Give it to Millicent the next time you find her (she'll be near her current location or advance to the Mountaintops). Exhaust her dialogue again after giving it.

  **8. Find Millicent at the Drainage Channel in Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree.**
  This is the deep late-game area. To get here: obtain both halves of the Haligtree Secret Medallion (left half from Albus in Village of the Albinaurics, Liurnia; right half from Commander Niall at Castle Sol in the Mountaintops). Use the completed medallion at the Grand Lift of Rold to reach the Consecrated Snowfield, then find the Hidden Path to the Haligtree (invisible walkways — use the map) and continue to Miquella's Haligtree, then Elphael. Find Millicent at the Drainage Channel site of grace in Elphael.

  **9. Complete her final quest — assist or invade.**
  When you exhaust Millicent's dialogue at the Drainage Channel, a summon sign and a hostile red sign appear nearby. You must choose:
  - **Assist her (golden sign):** Fight off the four invaders that appear. After winning, exhaust Millicent's final dialogue. She gives you the Unalloyed Gold Needle and dies peacefully.
  - **Invade her (red sign):** Kill Millicent. She drops the Unalloyed Gold Needle as a guaranteed drop.

  Either path gives you the Unalloyed Gold Needle. The quest achievement triggers on whichever choice you make.

  **10. (Optional) Use the Needle to undo the Frenzied Flame.**
  Take the Unalloyed Gold Needle to Malenia's arena (the boss room in Elphael) after defeating her. Use the needle where Malenia fell — it transforms into Miquella's Needle. **Miquella's Needle, used at the Dragonlord Placidusax boss arena in Crumbling Farum Azula, is the only way to undo the Frenzied Flame ending lock** if you embraced it during Hyetta's quest.

  **⚠️ Missable:** The Valkyrie's Prosthesis must be given to Millicent before she reaches Elphael — if you skip it, she won't advance. Sellia's seals and Gowry's repair step can easily be missed if you skip directly to the Church of the Plague.

### Nepheli Loux 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_NPC_NEPHELI`
- **title:** Complete Nepheli Loux's questline
- **method:** Nepheli is a warrior NPC whose quest runs parallel to the Kenneth Haight questline and ends with them co-ruling Limgrave.

  **1. Meet Nepheli below Stormveil Castle.**
  Nepheli is found as a summon before the Godrick boss fight and as an NPC in a room below the Roundtable Hold (accessible via the back staircase past the twin maidens). Exhaust her dialogue.

  **2. Give her the Stormhawk King ashes.**
  The Stormhawk King ashes are in a chest on the upper ramparts of Stormveil Castle (near the Site of Grace at the chapel). Give them to Nepheli — she will be visibly moved by this. **Do not give these to the Twin Maiden Husks before giving them to Nepheli or you cannot complete this step.**

  **3. Find Nepheli in the Village of the Albinaurics.**
  After certain story progression, Nepheli moves to the Village of the Albinaurics in southwest Liurnia (the poison swamp area under a cliff). She is sitting in despair near the entrance. Exhaust her dialogue.

  Speak to Sir Gideon Ofnir at the Roundtable Hold about Nepheli. He will hint at a path forward.

  **5. Find Nepheli at the Godrick throne room.**
  After receiving the Stormhawk King ashes and exhausting all dialogue, Nepheli appears at the throne room in Stormveil Castle (where Godrick was fought). Kenneth Haight also appears there. Exhaust both their dialogues.

  **6. Complete Kenneth Haight's questline.**
  Kenneth needs Fort Haight cleared of enemies and then must be spoken to again at Fort Haight. After he departs for Stormveil, both questlines resolve together — Nepheli and Kenneth jointly rule Limgrave from the throne. Speak to them to receive your reward (a Fingertip Seal) and complete both quests.

### Sorceress Sellen 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_NPC_SELLEN`
- **title:** Complete Sorceress Sellen's questline
- **method:** Sellen was a master sorcerer expelled from the Academy. Her questline involves finding her true body and choosing a side in a conflict between two NPCs.

  **1. Meet Sellen at the Waypoint Ruins.**
  Sellen is in a basement room at the Waypoint Ruins in western Limgrave (behind the Mad Pumpkin Head boss room). She acts as a sorcery trainer. Exhaust her dialogue until she mentions her past.

  **2. Find Azur at Mt. Gelmir and receive Comet Azur.**
  Master Azur sits in a hidden alcove on the cliffs of Mt. Gelmir (northeast of Volcano Manor, accessible by following the cliff path past the Erdtree-Gazing Hill in Altus). Exhaust his dialogue to receive the legendary sorcery Comet Azur. Return to Sellen and show it to her — she gives you her Primal Glintstone (her soul, essentially).

  **3. Find Sellen's real body in Witchbane Ruins.**
  Sellen's true body is imprisoned in the Witchbane Ruins in the Weeping Peninsula (southwest of the Fourth Church of Marika). **Do not kill this body.** Interact with it and use the Primal Glintstone.

  **4. Find Sellen's third location — Sellia Hideaway.**
  The cave Sellia Hideaway is in northeastern Caelid (hidden behind an illusory wall on the cliffs north of Sellia, Town of Sorcery). Navigate through the cave to find another imprisoned sorcerer, Lusat. Exhaust Lusat's dialogue (he gives you Stars of Ruin, another legendary sorcery — **while you're here**, pick it up).   - **Help Jerren:** Fight Sellen as a boss (she becomes a mass of glintstone anyway). Jerren rewards you with Ancient Dragon Smithing Stone and Eccentric's Armor.

### Shardbearer Malenia 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_BOSS_MALENIA`
- **title:** Beat Malenia, Blade of Miquella at the Haligtree in Elphael
- **method:** Malenia is widely considered the hardest boss in the game. Rivers of Blood (bleed) and Mimic Tear are the most common strategies for less experienced builds. **⚠️ She is completely optional** for story progression — there is no ending or other achievement that requires her death except this one and the Legendary Ashen Remains (Cleanrot Knight Finlay ash is in her dungeon, not dropped by her).

### God-Slaying Armament 🟢
**Steam achievement ID:** `ACH_MILESTONE_GODSLAYING`
