# Red Dead Redemption 2 — Achievement Guide

> Status: 🟢 done | 🔴 todo — **All 51 achievements documented ✅**

51 Steam achievements. The story milestones are all unmissable, but four achievements can be permanently lost mid-playthrough — read the **Missables** section before you start. The Online section requires a Rockstar account; most are low-effort and unlockable at low rank.

---

## Missables (Do These Before They're Gone)

These four achievements lock out permanently if you miss their windows. Everything else is available in free roam or can be earned during Epilogue.

---

### Lending a Hand
**Complete all optional Honor story missions.**

There are 28 optional Honor missions spread across Chapters 2–6. They do **not** carry over into free roam and cannot be triggered via Mission Replay — you must do them live during the correct chapter.

**Chapter 2 (Horseshoe Overlook):**
- *Money Lending and Other Sins I, II, III* — Collect debts for Leopold Strauss. Accept all three loansharking jobs as they become available and follow through.
- *Good, Honest, Snake Oil* — Stranger mission from a traveling snake oil salesman east of Flatneck Station.
- *We Loved Once and True I, II, III* — Help Beau Gray and Penelope Braithwaite elope. Start by speaking to Beau at his family's property in the Scarlett Meadows area.

**Chapter 3 (Clemens Point):**
- *The Course of True Love I, II, III* — Continuation of the Beau/Penelope story. Pick up Part I by speaking to Beau again.
- *Money Lending and Other Sins IV* — Another debt collection job from Strauss.

**Chapter 4 (Shady Belle):**
- *Help a Brother Out* — Speak to Brother Dorkins at the Saint Denis Cathedral after completing The Joys of Civilization.
- *Brothers and Sisters, One and All* — Continuation of the Brother Dorkins mission strand.
- *Fatherhood and Other Dreams I, II* — Speak to a man named Hamish Sinclair. Starts after a letter arrives at camp. Read it in your satchel to trigger.
- *Money Lending and Other Sins V* — Final Strauss debt job in Chapter 4.

**Chapter 6 (Beaver Hollow):**
- *The Course of True Love IV, V* — A letter from Penelope Braithwaite arrives at camp. Read it to trigger the final parts of her questline.
- *Archaeology for Beginners* — Speak to Rains Fall at the Wapiti Indian Reservation.
- *Honor, Amongst Thieves* — Speak to Captain Monroe at the Wapiti Indian Reservation. Requires positive honor.
- *Money Lending and Other Sins VI, VII* — Strauss sends you on two final jobs. Accept and complete them before the end of Chapter 6.
- *Do Not Seek Absolution I, II* — Visit Edith Downes in Annesburg. Requires at least Honor Level 4 (high honor). Very easy to miss — check your honor bar before Chapter 6 gets too far along.
- *Mrs. Sadie Adler, Widow I, II* — Triggered automatically during Chapter 6 story progression. Follow Sadie's quest to completion.

**Pro tip:** Keep your honor firmly in positive territory throughout the game. Several of the Chapter 6 missions are locked behind high honor, and letting it slip means locking yourself out.

### Friends With Benefits
**Complete a Companion Activity in each camp.**

The gang moves through three main camps: Horseshoe Overlook (Ch. 2–3), Clemens Point (Ch. 3), and Shady Belle/Beaver Hollow (Ch. 4–6). You need at least one companion activity completed per camp. Miss any camp entirely and the achievement is gone.

Activities appear as icons on your camp minimap — look for speech bubbles near gang members. Hunting and fishing trips, card games at the table, and robbery rides all count. Some are time-of-day restricted (card games typically only run in the evening).

**Recommended easy activities per camp:**
- **Horseshoe Overlook:** Play poker at the table with gang members (evening), or accept a hunting trip from Charles.
- **Clemens Point:** Fish with Javier (he'll be near the water), or hunt with Charles again.
- **Shady Belle / Beaver Hollow:** Hunt with Charles or ride out with another willing gang member.

**Note:** The Charles bank robbery companion activity requires the Special/Ultimate Edition DLC. It still counts if you have it, but don't count on it if you're on standard edition.

---

### Errand Boy
**Deliver 5 camp companion item requests.**

Gang members occasionally ask Arthur to fetch them specific items — whiskey, tobacco, medicine, etc. These requests appear as small icons at camp. You need to fulfill 5 of them across any camps.

Check in with gang members regularly, especially during Chapters 2–4 when camp is most active. If you've been doing companion activities and keeping camp morale up (donating to the tithing box), requests appear more frequently. Don't ignore camp — this one can slip by if you fast-travel everywhere.

---

### Give to the Poor
**Donate $250 to the gang tithing box.**

The tithing box sits in the center of every camp (look for the lockbox near Dutch's tent). Donate money to it whenever you can — $250 total across the whole game, not in one sitting. Loot everything, rob homesteads, and drop money into the box regularly from Chapter 2 onward. You'll hit $250 comfortably before Chapter 6 if you're consistent.

### Trusty Steed
**Reach max bonding level with a horse.**

Bond Level 4 is the maximum. Ride your horse consistently, feed it, brush it, and pat it regularly. Avoid letting it die — switching horses resets your bond with the new one. If you pick up a wild horse or buy one early and stick with it through the story, you'll hit Bond 4 naturally by mid-game.

## Open World & Collectibles

---

### Zoologist
**Study every animal across all states in Story Mode.**

162 animal species must be studied. Study an animal by aiming at it with your weapon and pressing the study button before killing it — or observe it from close range with your binoculars. Most animals are found naturally through exploration but a handful are region-specific or rare.

**Key tips:**
- Guarma (Chapter 5) has unique animals, but they are **not required** for this achievement. Don't stress about studying everything there.
- Fish count — study them by equipping your rod and getting close, or just catch them.
- Legendary animals count. Study them before killing.
- Use Eagle Eye to spot animals in tall grass or at a distance.
- The Naturalist role in Online is separate — this is Story Mode only.

Use a checklist (PowerPyx has a complete 162-animal list with locations) and tick off each species. Late-game animals in Tall Trees, Roanoke Ridge, and Bayou Nwa are commonly missed.

---

### Skin Deep
**Skin every species of animal in Story Mode.**

160 species must be skinned. Same animals as Zoologist but excludes some that can only be studied (like fish). Kill and skin them — don't collect the carcass intact. This pairs naturally with Zoologist; study first, then skin.

**Important:** Some animals like the Legendary Panther (Giaguaro) only appear after completing specific Master Hunter challenges. Plan ahead — don't finish the game before unlocking all challenge-gated animals.

---

### Paying Respects
**Find the graves of your fallen gang members.**

All 9 graves are only accessible **after completing the full story including both epilogues**. Visit each one and interact to pay respects.

**All 9 grave locations:**
1. **Jenny Kirk** — Northwest of the map, east of Spider Gorge riverbank near Colter.
2. **Davey Callander** — Church graveyard in Colter (same area as the Prologue).
3. **Sean MacGuire** — Flat Iron Lake shoreline, on the cliffs above the lake west of Rhodes.
4. **Kieran Duffy** — West of Shady Belle, south of Bolger Glade in the meadows.
5. **Hosea Matthews** — Southeast Bluewater Marsh, under a cluster of trees in the swamp.
6. **Lenny Summers** — Southeast Bluewater Marsh, near Hosea's grave under the same trees.
7. **Susan Grimshaw** — On the hill overlooking the railroad, southwest of Elysian Pool.
8. **Eagle Flies** — West of Donner Falls, on a cliff face overlooking the waterfall.
9. **Arthur Morgan** — East of Donner Falls in Ambarino, near the Mysterious Hill Home. Climb the hill to the first overlook. Note: Arthur's grave inscription changes based on your honor — high honor shows a more respectful epitaph.

### Artificial Intelligence
**Discover the fate of Marko Dragic.**

This is a two-part stranger mission called *A Bright Bouncing Boy*.

**Part 1:** Find Marko Dragic standing at the pond in the western Saint Denis park during **daytime**. He wants to demonstrate his remote-controlled boat to investors. Help him complete the demonstration.

**Part 2:** Wait at least 2 in-game days, then visit Marko's lab in the northeastern corner of the map (northeast of Doverhill, near the Roanoke Valley area) during **nighttime**. You'll find the conclusion to his story and unlock the achievement.

This mission becomes available in Chapter 4 after completing *The Joys of Civilization*.

---

### It's Art
**Find a permanent home for the squirrel statue.**

Meet the Hamish Sinclair stranger in Flatneck Station area (he carves wooden animals). He'll give you an ugly squirrel statue. Donate it to the art gallery in Saint Denis — visit the gallery and find the option to donate the piece. The achievement pops when the statue is accepted.

This stranger is available from Chapter 2 onward.

---

### Collector's Item
**Complete one of the Collectable strands.**

Three collectable sets exist in Story Mode: Dinosaur Bones, Rock Carvings, and Dreamcatchers. Complete any one of them fully. Rock Carvings (the *Geology for Beginners* stranger quest from Francis Sinclair, available from Chapter 2 northwest of Strawberry) is the most popular choice — there are 10 carvings total, and each one requires mailing coordinates to Sinclair. After mailing all 10, visit Sinclair's cabin to collect your reward and unlock the achievement.

---

### Western Stranger
**Complete 10 Stranger mission strands.**

Stranger missions appear as white question marks on the map. There are more than 10 available, so you have flexibility. Prioritize multi-part strands (they count once fully completed, not per part). The Marko Dragic and Francis Sinclair missions count. Most are available from Chapters 2–4 onward and remain accessible through the Epilogue.

## Gameplay & Challenges

---

### Best in the West
**Attain 100% completion.**

The most demanding achievement in the game. 100% requires completing a specific checklist tracked under Pause Menu > Progress > Total Completion. Key categories:

- **Story missions** — all main story missions completed
- **Stranger missions** — complete the required number of stranger strands (not all, just the ones in the tracker)
- **Chance encounters** — random world events (robberies, ambushes, people in distress) — many are missable per chapter
- **Bounty hunting** — complete bounty missions in each region
- **Gang hideouts** — clear each one
- **Hunting** — complete Master Hunter challenges
- **Fishing** — catch the required fish species
- **Collectibles** — find enough dinosaur bones, rock carvings, and dreamcatchers to satisfy the tracker (not 100% of each, just the required number)
- **Mini games** — play each mini game at least once
- **Challenges** — complete all challenge sets

**Critical note:** The Lending a Hand missions, companion activities, and item requests are **not** part of the 100% completion tracker — but their related story missions may be. Focus on the tracker categories above. Use the PowerPyx 100% checklist to cross-reference what's actually required.

---

### Gold Rush
**Earn 70 Gold Medals in Story missions.**

There are 104 gold-medal-eligible missions, so you only need 70 — pick the easiest ones. Gold medals require completing all optional objectives in a single playthrough of a mission (e.g., complete in under a time limit, don't use health items, get a certain number of headshots).

**Key tips:**
- All missions can be replayed via Pause Menu > Progress > Story — but replays temporarily replace your weapons with a default loadout, which can make some missions harder.
- Early Chapter 2 missions are easy golds — replay them if you missed objectives first time.
- Before replaying, eat a cooked Thyme Big Game meal for the stamina boost (you can't eat during replays).
- Avoid missions with tight time limits if you find them stressful — there are enough straightforward golds to hit 70 without them.
- The final Chapter 6 mission is very difficult to gold — skip it.

---

### Extreme Personality
**Reach maximum or minimum Honor level.**

Honor ranges from -8 (Dishonorable) to +8 (Honorable). Either extreme unlocks the achievement. Easiest method: play the story normally with good behavior (spare enemies, greet strangers, help people) and you'll trend toward max honor naturally. If you want dishonorable, shoot civilians, rob everyone, and antagonize people.

High honor is recommended regardless — it unlocks the *Do Not Seek Absolution* missions needed for Lending a Hand, and Arthur's story is richer at high honor.

---

### Grin and Bear It
**Survive 18 bear attacks and kill the bear each time in Story Mode.**

This requires grizzly bears specifically — black bears do not count. The bear must knock Arthur to the ground and trigger the ground struggle button prompt. After fighting it off, shoot the bear while still on the ground to register the kill.

**Best farming spot:** The area halfway between Wallace Station and Watson's Cabin, north of Little Creek River in West Elizabeth. Grizzlies spawn here consistently.

**Farming method:** Kill a bear, then ride away for about a minute. Set up camp and sleep 1–2 days. Ride back — a new bear should have spawned. Repeat 18 times. This takes 1–2 hours of patient grinding.

---

### Bountiful
**Survive 3 days holding a bounty of $250 in all states.**

You need a $250 bounty simultaneously across all five states (New Hanover, Lemoyne, West Elizabeth, Ambarino, New Austin) and survive — not get arrested or killed — for 3 in-game days.

**Method:** Go on a rampage in multiple states to rack up bounties, then hide in your camp or a secluded area and sleep through 3 days. Avoid lawmen entirely during this window. Wearing your bandana and keeping off roads helps. Once 3 days have passed, you can pay off the bounties at any post office.

This is easiest to do in the Epilogue when you have more money and map access.

---

### It Was THIS Big!
**Catch a fish weighing at least 16 lbs (7.3 kg).**

Large fish spawn at specific fishing spots. The easiest reliable location is **Lake Owanjila** west of Strawberry — use Special Lake Lure (available from the bait shop in Lagras or Emerald Ranch) to target the large muskie there. The legendary muskie at this location weighs well over 16 lbs.

Alternatively, target the legendary fish in any major lake using the Special Lures. Legendary fish are massive and always qualify.

---

### Breaking and Entering
**Recover the stash from 4 homesteads.**

Homestead robberies are world events where you encounter a house that can be burgled. Some require returning at night, some involve intimidating the occupants. Four total are needed.

**Known homestead stash locations:**
- Chez Porter (northeast of Emerald Ranch)
- Lonnie's Shack (southeast of Flatneck Station, best done at night)
- Ridgewood Farm (south of Flatneck Station)
- Catfish Jacksons (southwest of Braithwaite Manor, available in Ch. 3 onward)
- Hanging Dog Ranch (northwest of Wallace Station — cleared as part of a stranger mission too)

---

### Self Sufficient
**Craft 30 unique items in Story Mode.**

Open your satchel and craft items from the crafting menu using herbs, animal fat, and other materials. Tonics, ammunition, and camp provisions all count. Gather plants consistently during exploration and you'll have enough materials to hit 30 unique crafted items well before the end of the story. Focus on crafting different item types rather than multiples of the same thing — the requirement is 30 *unique* items.

---

### Hobby Horse
**Play all mini games.**

Five mini games must be played at least once: Poker, Blackjack, Dominoes, Five Finger Fillet, and Arm Wrestling. Locations:

- **Poker** — Flatneck Station (available from Ch. 2), Tumbleweed Saloon (Epilogue)
- **Blackjack** — Van Horn Trading Post saloon, Rhodes saloon
- **Dominoes** — Saint Denis park bench, Emerald Ranch
- **Five Finger Fillet** — Flatneck Station, Van Horn, Strawberry
- **Arm Wrestling** — Flatneck Station, Van Horn

You don't need to win — just play each game at least once.

---

### Locked and Loaded
**Upgrade each available component for a single sidearm or longarm weapon.**

Visit a gunsmith (Rhodes, Saint Denis, Valentine, Strawberry, Van Horn, or Blackwater) and fully upgrade one weapon. Components include barrel, stock, grip, sights, and various finishes. The cheapest route is to pick a pistol or rifle you already own and purchase every available upgrade for it in one sitting. The Cattleman Revolver is widely available from the start and cheap to upgrade fully.

---

### Take From the Rich
**Rob or loot $250.**

Loot every enemy you kill, rob travelers on the road, and break into homesteads. $250 cumulative across the whole game — you'll hit this naturally by Chapter 3 without trying.

---

### Pony Up
**Spend $5000 across all shops.**

$5000 total across any shops — clothing stores, gunsmiths, general stores, stables. Buy ammunition, food, upgrades, and clothes as you play. You'll need active spending habits but it's achievable by the Epilogue without grinding. If short, buy and sell items at the fence or stock up on expensive ammo types.

## Online

RDR2 Online requires a Rockstar Social Club account linked to your Steam account. Most Online achievements are low-effort and unlock through casual play. Online is still active as of 2025 but the player population is reduced — finding Posse members for some achievements may require the RDR2 Discord community.

---

### Breakout
**Red Dead Online: Complete the Intro.**

Finish the short Online tutorial mission. Unmissable if you launch Online mode.

---

### Getting Started
**Red Dead Online: Reach Rank 10.**

Play the Online mode — complete missions, hunt, and do free roam activities. Rank 10 comes quickly with a few hours of play.

---

### Notorious
**Red Dead Online: Reach Rank 50.**

A significant time investment — Rank 50 requires consistent Online play. Focus on story missions, free roam missions, and Showdown Series events for XP. Daily challenges also give solid XP bonuses. Plan on 15–20+ hours of Online play.

---

### Strength in Numbers
**Red Dead Online: Complete a Free Roam mission as part of a Posse with at least 2 members.**

Form or join a Posse (temporary or persistent) and complete any Free Roam mission together. Open the Player menu to form a Posse, then call a mission from any NPC. Two players total is the minimum — grab one friend or find a stranger in the lobby.

---

### Posse Up
**Red Dead Online: Form a Persistent Posse.**

Persistent Posses cost $200 in-game gold (the in-game currency, not Gold Bars). Open the Player menu, go to Posse, and select Create Persistent Posse. You don't need members to form one — just pay the fee.

---

### Buckle Up
**Red Dead Online: Achieve 5 gold belt buckles from awards.**

Awards are challenges tracked in the Player menu under Awards. Completing award tiers earns belt buckles. Focus on awards tied to things you're already doing — kills, hunting, fishing, mission completions. Five buckles require 5 award completions at any tier.

---

### Butchered
**Red Dead Online: Sell 20 items to the Butcher.**

The Butcher is an NPC found in most Online towns (Thieves' Landing, Blackwater, Rhodes, etc.). Hunt animals and sell the pelts and carcasses to the Butcher. 20 individual item sales — a couple of hunting sessions will cover this.

---

### Picked to Perfection
**Red Dead Online: Pick 25 Herbs.**

Herbs are scattered throughout the Online map just like Story Mode. Ride through fields and forest areas pressing the interact button near glowing plants. 25 herbs takes under 30 minutes.

---

### Gun For Hire
**Red Dead Online: Accept 10 Free Roam missions from characters around the world.**

Free Roam missions are offered by NPCs with mission icons on the Online map (Cripps, Horley, Marshall Davies, etc.). Ride to 10 different mission givers and accept their jobs. You don't need to complete them with exceptional results — just accept and finish 10.

---

### Master Craftsman
**Red Dead Online: Craft 20 items (excluding ammo).**

Open the crafting menu in Online (via campfire or crafting table) and craft 20 non-ammo items — provisions, tonics, and equipment all count. Gather herbs and animal materials and craft through your available recipes.

---

### Series Major
**Red Dead Online: Take part in a Series.**

A Series is a curated playlist of competitive events (Showdown Series, etc.). Open the Online menu and look for an active Series — join and complete one event. These rotate on Rockstar's schedule.

---

### Eventful
**Red Dead Online: Play 5 Free Roam Events.**

Free Roam Events trigger automatically in the Online world on a timer — a notification will appear on screen. Join any 5 of them. Events include target shooting competitions, animal hunts, and race-style activities. Just be in the lobby when they trigger and join.

---

### All's Fair
**Red Dead Online: Successfully counter a rival Posse's Free Roam mission.**

This requires some coordination. When a rival Posse is running a Free Roam mission nearby, you'll get a prompt to counter it. Accept the counter and complete the objective (usually protecting or stealing something from them). Best done with friends — a rival Posse in a populated lobby is needed. Use the RDR2 community Discord if you're struggling to find organic opponents.

---

### Home Comforts
**Red Dead Online: Purchase 5 camp improvements.**

Open the Online camp menu (via Cripps at your camp) and buy improvements — a dog, a flag, a stove, ammunition wagon upgrades, etc. Some cost in-game dollars, some cost Gold Bars. Five total. Stock up on in-game currency through missions and hunting before shopping.

---

### Non-Regulation
**Red Dead Online: Craft 25 pieces of ammunition.**

Craft 25 ammo items via the crafting menu. Basic ammo types are cheap — gather materials and craft batches of arrows or express cartridges. This is quick and easy alongside Master Craftsman.

---

### Horses for Courses
**Red Dead Online: Concurrently own 5 horses.**

Buy 5 horses from the Online stable (or capture wild horses and stable them). You need to own all 5 at the same time. Cheaper horses work fine — Morgan, Tennessee Walker, and Mustang are inexpensive. This requires a reasonable amount of Online currency but is achievable at mid-rank.

---

### The Real Deal
**Red Dead Online: Achieve MVP 3 times (in a round with at least 4 players).**

MVP is awarded to the top performer at the end of a Showdown Series match. You need to be the best player in a match with at least 4 participants, 3 separate times. Focus on team deathmatch-style modes where you can rack up kills. Practice your aim and play aggressively. This is the toughest Online achievement — it requires genuine skill or a friendly lobby arrangement.
